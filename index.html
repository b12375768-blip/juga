The 60-second pre-upload check

Run this before every upload. It catches the overwhelming majority of copyright problems.

  • Can I name the owner of every piece of music and footage in the video?
  • Do I have the licence or written permission saved somewhere I can find in 60 seconds?
  • Does my licence cover this platform, this type of use, and monetisation?
  • Is the length of the excerpt within what the licence allows?
  • Am I using the track commercially (ads, sponsors, Patreon) if the licence is non-commercial?

Still confused? Start with these

The three questions we hear most often, answered without hedging.

  • "Can I use this song if it's only 10 seconds?"
    Duration is one input among several, not a free pass. A 10-second sample in a monetised video can still be a claim.
  • "I credited the artist — am I safe?"
    Credit is a courtesy. It does not create permission and does not defeat a claim.
  • "It was a Content ID claim, not a strike — am I fine?"
    Usually, yes for your channel's survival, but claims can block monetisation in some regions and can escalate into a strike.
