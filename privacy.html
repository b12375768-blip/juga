Privacy Policy

The short version: reading this site collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, and no newsletter. This page explains that in full.

Overview

Creator Rights ("we", "us") publishes free educational guides about copyright for creators. The guiding principle is that the reading experience should not require you to hand over anything. This policy explains what we do collect, why, and what we do not collect.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile to create.
  • No newsletter, no email list. We do not run a mailing list and will not ask you to subscribe.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers. We do not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site behaviour trackers.
  • No data from the interactive check. Your answers in the Copyright Risk Check are processed entirely in your browser and are never transmitted or stored.
  • No payment data. There is nothing to buy on this site.

We do not sell data, share it for advertising, or build profiles of visitors. If you send us a message, we use it to reply and nothing else.

Local storage

The site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The interactive risk check stores nothing at all — if you close the tab, your answers are gone. Your browser may store standard technical data such as cache and session state; that is under your browser's control and can be cleared through its settings.

Third parties

Two external services appear on this site:

  • Google Fonts — supplies the typeface. Your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Your host — serves the files and generates the access logs described above.

No other third-party scripts, embeds, trackers or widgets are loaded. In particular, we do not embed content from video platforms or social networks, which would otherwise allow them to track visitors as soon as a page loaded.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In practice, the only personal data we are likely to hold is a message you sent us and an IP address in a log file.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the form on the contact page. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Children

This site is general-audience educational content and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age, through the reading experience.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We apply the same standard of care we would expect of any small independent site — which is realistic but not absolute. Please do not send us confidential or sensitive material through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of the page. We will not add new data practices without making them visible here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent via the contact page.
