The Copyright Risk Check

Ten questions about a video you are planning to publish. Pick the answer that is actually true for your project and you get the reason straight away, plus a link to the guide that covers it. Two minutes, and it tells you whether to press publish or fix something first.

10 questions Instant result Guidance per answer Nothing stored

How to use it

Have a specific upload in mind — a video, a Short, a podcast episode, a stream. Answer honestly rather than ideally: the check is only useful if it describes the video you are actually making. There are no wrong answers, and every answer explains itself the moment you pick it.

This is education, not a verdict

A "low risk" result is not a guarantee, and a "high risk" result is not a legal opinion. For commercial projects or live disputes, get advice from a qualified attorney in your jurisdiction.
