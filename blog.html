One more thing

The three sentences that prevent most disasters

  1. Track where it came from. If you cannot name the source and the licence of every third-party element, you do not know whether you are allowed to use it.
  2. Keep the evidence. A licence PDF in a folder named by project beats reconstructing permission from memory during a dispute.
  3. Re-check when the video changes. Monetisation, sponsorships and new platforms can push a use outside what your licence allows.
